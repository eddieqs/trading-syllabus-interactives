Implied rate sits inside your band. No trade.

Push the call price up and watch the implied rate climb through your borrowing rate: the conversion appears. Push the put up and the reversal appears, unless you raise the borrow fee first, in which case it never does, because only the reversal shorts the share. Now switch on "I can lend the shares" and raise the fee: the conversion bound drops too, and the same rich put that no one else can arbitrage becomes a good lend for you. The implied borrow line reads the fee straight out of the option prices.
