Derivatives foundations

Interactive dashboards

Three models from The financing spine of options. Each runs entirely in the page — move the sliders and watch the band move.

Section 3 — the forward is a band

Cash and Carry Band

Set the funding spread and borrow fee to zero and the band collapses to the classroom forward. Widen them and watch how far the market forward must move before either trade pays. On a hard-to-borrow name, push the fee to 10% and see which side of the band disappears.

Section 6 — calls, puts, one identity

Synthetic Forward Builder

Turn the legs on and off. With both on, the sum is a single straight line crossing zero at K with no kink anywhere — a forward. These are payoffs at expiry, not profits: premiums paid or received are not shown.

Section 8 — the loan inside the options

Conversion and Reversal Band

Push the call price up and watch the implied rate climb through your borrowing rate: the conversion appears. Push the put up and the reversal appears — unless you raise the borrow fee first, because only the reversal shorts the share. Switch on “I can lend the shares” and raise the fee: the conversion bound drops too.
